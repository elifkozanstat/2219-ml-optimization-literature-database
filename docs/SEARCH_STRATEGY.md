# Search Strategy and Evidence Rules

## Review objective
The database was assembled to map optimization and learning approaches relevant to the project's engineering application and to provide an auditable evidence base for WP1.

## Search clusters
- adaptive and gradient-based ML optimization;
- robust and adaptive losses;
- metaheuristic and engineering optimization;
- learned shortest-path/distance indexes on road networks;
- travel-time and coordinate-based operational estimation.

## Time emphasis
Recent work from 2024–September 2026 was deliberately emphasized. Earlier studies were retained when methodologically foundational or necessary to trace the development of the field.

## Preferred evidence sources
Official journal and conference pages, proceedings, institutional repositories, and author-hosted papers were preferred. Preprints were used only where directly relevant or uniquely current and are explicitly marked.

## Inclusion logic
A record was retained when it contributed directly to at least one of:
1. optimization procedure or adaptive gradient method;
2. objective/loss-function design;
3. robustness or heavy-tail handling;
4. engineering/metaheuristic optimization;
5. learned road-network distance/time estimation.

Enough information had to be available to extract bibliographic and methodological details without guessing.

## Evidence-depth rule
The database distinguishes between full/detailed-source checking and abstract/metadata checking. This prevents the public artifact from implying that every record received identical full-text review.

## Scope statement
This is a structured state-of-the-art database. It should not be described as an exhaustive systematic review or meta-analysis.

## Workflow status
The evidence workflow was completed in three stages: bibliography/taxonomy construction, detailed checking of priority sources, and use of the verified fields in D1.1, D1.2, D1.3, and D1.4. Formal D1.3 scores are maintained in the separate D1.3 deliverable and are not duplicated in this database.
